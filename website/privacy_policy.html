1. Introduction

FinSight is a personal expense tracking and budgeting application intended for the owner and a limited group of invited users.

This policy explains how FinSight handles your personal information when you use its expenses, budgets, analytics, and AI chatbot features.

2

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use FinSight, we may process:

person
Account information Name, email address, and account details.
receipt_long
Financial information Expenses, amounts, dates, categories, merchants, payment methods, and budgets you enter.
chat
Chatbot information Your messages, conversation history, and relevant financial information used to answer your questions.
devices
Technical information Basic device, browser, IP address, and application activity information required to operate and protect the service.
warning
Important Security Notice: Please do not enter bank passwords, card PINs, CVVs, UPI PINs, or OTPs into FinSight.
3

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Manage your account and access.
  • Record and organise your expenses.
  • Calculate budgets, summaries, and analytics.
  • Answer questions through the AI chatbot.
  • Process expense or budget changes you request.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the application.
verified We do not sell your personal or financial information.
4

4. AI Chatbot and Groq

FinSight uses Groq to generate AI chatbot responses.

When you ask a question, relevant messages and financial information may be sent to Groq to generate a response. Conversation information may also be stored to support follow-up questions.

info AI responses may sometimes be inaccurate. Please verify important information before relying on it.

Groq's data handling is subject to its own policies:

  • open_in_new Groq Privacy Policy
  • open_in_new Groq Data Processing Addendum
  • open_in_new Groq Data Usage Documentation
5

5. Data Storage and Sharing

FinSight uses Supabase for storing application and chatbot data. The Flask backend processes application requests.

Information may also be processed by service providers required to operate the application, including hosting, database, authentication, and AI providers.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers for advertising purposes.

Your financial records and chatbot conversations are intended to remain private to your account. The operator may access information when reasonably necessary for maintenance, support, or security.

6

6. Data Retention

We follow these intended retention periods:

Financial records Until you delete them or close your account. Following account deletion, removal from active systems is intended within 30 days.
Chatbot messages 12 months after your last activity, or until account deletion.
Chatbot checkpoints 30 days after the associated chat session ends.
Account records While your account is active; removal within 30 days after account deletion, except records required by law.
Security logs 180 days, unless longer retention is necessary for legal or security reasons.
Backups Intended to expire within 30 days after the corresponding data is deleted from active systems.
7

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to:

  • Request information about your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request account closure and data deletion.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.

We may verify your identity before processing a request. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law.

8

8. Cookies and Browser Storage

FinSight may use cookies or browser storage for login sessions, application functionality, and preferences such as light or dark mode.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, and misuse. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

For more information, please read our Security page.
